Buffalo Chicken Bites

These lactose-free Buffalo Chicken Bites are crispy, tangy, and full of flavor. Juicy chicken is fried to golden perfection, tossed in spicy Buffalo sauce, and served with a cool, creamy garlic ranch. It’s a crowd-pleasing classic you’ll want in your game-day lineup.

Ingredients

For the Buffalo Chicken Bites

  • 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 cups Buffalo sauce
  • 3 large eggs

For the Marinade

  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tablespoons hot sauce
  • 2 teaspoons garlic salt
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 cups LACTAID Whole Milk
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup pickle juice

For the Garlic Ranch

  • 1 cup LACTAID Sour Cream
  • 3 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons chives, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2-3 tablespoons LACTAID Whole Milk
  • 1 pinch of Kosher salt
  • 1 tablespoon hot sauce
