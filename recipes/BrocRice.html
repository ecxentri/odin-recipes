Broccoli Rice soup

This creamy, lactose-free broccoli soup recipe is made with rice, broth, veggies and LACTAID® milk! You'll love how this hearty and healthy soup warms you up on a cold day.

Ingredients

  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, peeled and chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, chopped
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth

Directions

  1. In a soup pot, combine onion, carrot, celery, garlic and chicken broth, and bring to a boil.
  2. Add rice to the soup pot, cover and simmer for 20 minutes, or until rice is tender.
  3. Once rice is tender, add broccoli, cover and simmer and cook until brocolli is tender, about 5 minutes.
  4. Using either an immersion blender or a regular blender with and opening, process until smooth.
  5. Return to saucepan and add LACTAID Fat-Free Lactose-Free Milk, thyme, and pepper, and heat slowly. Do not boil.
  6. Serve hot or cold.
